## Set-wide fix

**No background disc, circle, plate, medallion or ring behind the symbol.** The
symbol stands alone on a transparent background, like `hello` and `_generic`. The
addon already draws a dark circular slot with a gold ring around every icon.

Make the subject **large and bold**: fill about 75% of the canvas and keep the
details simple enough to read at 40 px.
